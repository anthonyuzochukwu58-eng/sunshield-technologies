1 Introduction

Welcome to SunShield Technologies. We are committed to protecting your personal privacy and maintaining the absolute security of your private data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, request a site assessment, or purchase our solar power, smart inverter, and security camera systems.

By using our services, accessing our website, or submitting your information through our custom request forms, you consent to the data collection and management practices described in this document.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to provide you with customized solar power design blueprints, site assessments, and security system installations. This includes:

  • Personal Contact Information: Your full name, telephone number, email address, and physical property address for site audits.
  • Property & Energy Load Data: General details about your home or business, current monthly electricity expenditure, generator running costs, and appliance usage patterns.
  • Technical Usage Data: Your IP address, browser type, device details, and interaction metrics when navigating our online platform.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use your collected information strictly to fulfill our service delivery promises and optimize your customer experience. Your data is used to:

  • Conduct the paid or custom Site Assessment and design your personalized Solar & Backup Blueprint.
  • Schedule on-site visits and communicate energy analysis results with you directly.
  • Send system performance notifications, maintenance alerts, and security support updates.
  • Improve website functionality, analyze service metrics, and protect against fraudulent activities.

4 Sharing Your Data

We respect your privacy. SunShield Technologies does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketing companies. We only share relevant data with trusted partners under strict confidentiality agreements, including:

  • Technical Installation Teams: Authorized engineering partners who assist in setting up your physical solar and CCTV systems.
  • Legal Compliance: Government or regulatory bodies when required to do so by applicable Nigerian laws or the NDPR.

5 Data Security

We implement high-grade industrial security protocols to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All data collected via our website forms, including the custom site assessment request system, is encrypted during transmission.

While no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, we continuously update our digital barriers, security patches, and physical storage firewalls to match modern industry safety standards.

6 Your NDPR Rights

Under the Nigerian Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), you hold specific rights regarding how businesses process your personal data. These rights include:

  • The right to request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request immediate correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal profile details.
  • The right to request the deletion or complete erasure of your contact details from our records, provided there are no active service delivery contracts or legal holds.
  • The right to object to or withdraw consent for marketing messages.

7 Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies to improve loading speeds, analyze site traffic, and track user preferences. You can choose to disable cookies through your personal web browser settings, although doing so may restrict access to certain interactive elements of our platform.

8 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect updates in legal compliances, technological shifts, or operational changes. Any updates will be published immediately on this page with an updated "Effective Date" at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed.
